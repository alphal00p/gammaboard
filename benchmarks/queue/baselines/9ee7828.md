# Compact queue benchmark baseline

Benchmark code: `9ee7828`; engine code: `0ea7f08`. Built with locked dependencies,
`--profile dev-optim --no-default-features`. Executable hash and compact results
are in [9ee7828.json](9ee7828.json). These are measurements on a shared development
host, not expected rates for every machine. GL26 was paused for the baseline and
focused comparison, then resumed.

## Runtime and repeatability

- Full 16-case baseline: **99.1 seconds**.
- Full 32-measurement identical-binary A/B check: **206.3 seconds**.
- Focused burst check (three paired 20-second measurements): **153.6 seconds**.
- All times include deployment and cleanup; all stayed below five minutes.

The broad suite is a coarse screen: its identical-binary ratios ranged from
0.699 to 1.148.
The focused burst check was much more repeatable; its paired B/A ratios were
1.0010, 0.9990, 1.0067, with mean 1.0022.
Use focused repeated measurements to judge small changes. Neither check is an
optimization comparison: both sides used the same executable.

## Baseline

| Evaluators | Nominal samples/s | Steady samples/s | Burst samples/s | Completed burst updates | Mean update stall (s) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1,000 | 967.1 | 291.5 | 5 | 0.5001 |
| 1 | 2,000,000 | 616951.5 | 107573.5 | 4 | 0.5001 |
| 4 | 1,000 | 990.8 | 274.4 | 4 | 0.5001 |
| 4 | 2,000,000 | 611518.5 | 97839.9 | 4 | 0.5001 |
| 16 | 1,000 | 949.1 | 271.3 | 5 | 0.5001 |
| 16 | 2,000,000 | 522532.1 | 86405.1 | 4 | 0.5001 |
| 64 | 1,000 | 582.4 | 121.9 | 2 | 0.5001 |
| 64 | 2,000,000 | 661714.0 | 78334.8 | 4 | 0.5001 |

Burst generation has no artificial delay. After each finite window returns its
feedback, the sampler stops producing for a 0.5-second update, then starts the
next burst. The traces show production spikes, an empty queue during the update,
and recovery afterward. Every burst case completed at least two updates during
measurement. Feedback waiting and queue overhead add to the update delay.

Windows are `max(64, min(100000, ceil(nominal_rate * 0.25)))` points. The 100 ms
batch target and 100 ms observation interval make the short cases observable.
The 64-worker, 1,000/s case retains 64 ms per point: minimum batch sizes and small
windows limit its usable parallelism. These short-window rates should not be
interpreted as steady-state integrand performance.

Reproduce the focused check:

```sh
python3 scripts/benchmark_queue.py ab --baseline /path/to/A --candidate /path/to/B \
  --evaluators 4 --rates 1000 --regimes training_burst \
  --warmup 2 --duration 20 --repetitions 3 --output /tmp/burst-ab
```
